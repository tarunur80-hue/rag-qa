Excellence Tech Employee Policy Handbook

Section 1: Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees get a one-hour lunch break each day. Employees may work from home on Wednesdays and Fridays with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which all employees must be available for meetings.

Section 2: Leave Policy
Employees are allowed 20 days of paid leave annually. Leave accrues monthly and unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried forward to the next calendar year. Employees must apply for leave at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal. Leave of more than 5 consecutive days needs approval from both the manager and the HR department.

Section 3: Sick Leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave each year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward and does not count toward the 20 days of paid leave.

Section 4: Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays each year. The full holiday list is published on the HR portal in January. If a holiday falls on a weekend, no substitute day off is given.

Section 5: Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave. Both must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

Section 6: Expense Reimbursement
Employees can claim reimbursement for approved business travel, meals during client visits, and internet expenses. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense with original receipts. Meal expenses are capped at 1,000 rupees per day. Claims are processed within 10 working days.

Section 7: Performance Reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review covers goals achieved, teamwork, and skill development. Salary revisions are decided only after the December review.

Section 8: Resignation and Notice Period
Employees must give 60 days written notice when resigning. The notice period can be shortened only with written approval from HR. Final salary and documents are provided within 15 days after the last working day.
